Which AI subscription can I choose from?
Five subscriptions are currently available for selection. Take some time to consider your needs and select the AI subscription that best aligns with your learning goals.
1. ChatGPT Plus
Access to OpenAI's latest reasoning models, including GTP-5.6 at point of writing, with higher usage limits for usage compared to the free tier.
What you can use it for: Assist with coding, everyday work tasks such as drafting messages and creating slide decks, or in-depth analysis and research.
Learn more about ChatGPT here.
2. Google AI Pro
Expanded access to Google's AI tools—including Gemini, Nano Banana Pro, and Gemini Notebook—featuring 4x higher usage limits and zero daily caps. The plan also comes with 5TB cloud storage, and Google Health Premium.
What you can use it for: Integrate AI assistance seamlessly across Google apps using Gemini Agentic AI, such as accessing Gemini directly to help you proofread in Gmail and working smarter in Google Docs and Google Sheets. Plus, generate high-fidelity marketing visuals and creative assets with Nano Banana and Omni.
Learn more about Google AI Pro here.
Note: YouTube Premium Lite is not included in this redemption.
3. Manus AI
Your general AI agent for real knowledge work. The plan includes 8,000 credits (worth S$50) per month.
What you can use it for: Complex research, websites, presentation slides, image, video, data analysis and visualisations. Manus picks the right model for each job and ships quality work.
Learn more about Manus here.
4. Microsoft 365 Personal
Access Microsoft 365 apps such as Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook with built-in AI, plus 1TB of cloud storage.
What you can use it for: Leverage Copilot to draft documents, summarize information, generate presentations, and uncover insights in spreadsheets, helping you work smarter and save time.
Learn more about Microsoft 365 Personal here.
5. Singtel AI Pass
Access a curated suite of premium AI tools for three months, allowing you to explore and compare different capabilities before selecting one tool for a further three months to deepen your skills or work on more advanced projects.
What you can use it for: Go beyond a single AI tool and explore a broader range of AI capabilities with Manus, Canva, Hailuo AI, Otter.ai, MiniMax Code, MiniMax Audio and AKOOL, from capturing and summarising meetings to generating content, video and audio, automating workflows, and using AI agents for complex, multi-step tasks.
Learn more about Singtel AI Pass here.
Tip: Match the subscription to your goals. You can also try the free tier of each provider (where available) before deciding. Note that the free tier may not include all the features available as part of the premium subscriptions.

How do I redeem the free AI subscription?
1. Attend an eligible course
Attend any AI course carrying the "Free AI sub" label.
2. Receive notification
Get an email with redemption details after attending the course.
3. Submit the request form
Fill up and submit the form found in the redemption details email, indicating your preferred AI subscription.
4. Activate your AI subscription
Receive the request outcome and activation details (if successful) via email.
Note: You can redeem only one free AI subscription under the programme, regardless of the number of eligible AI courses you attend.
Frequently Asked Questions
Can I redeem more than one AI subscription?
No, each eligible Singapore Citizen aged 18 and above can only redeem one AI subscription, regardless of the number of eligible AI courses taken.
Can I change my mind of preferred AI subscription after submitting the request form?
No, you cannot change the selection of the free AI subscription once the request form is submitted.
Do I need to redeem my free AI subscription immediately after attending an eligible course?
No. You have 60 days from the date of the notification email to submit your request.
