FAQs

Can I try all the subjects during the trial?

Yes! Our 7-day trial includes full access to all available subjects – Maths, Science, Chinese and English. Just click “Try Now” above to explore everything KooBits has to offer.

Why do I need to choose a subject to start the trial if I get access to all 4?

We ask you to choose a subject so we can personalise your child’s learning experience from the start. During the free trial, your child can explore Maths, Science, Chinese and English. However, your paid subscription covers only the subject you choose to try, not all four.

We recommend choosing the subject your child needs the most help with so they can start with the most relevant learning content.

Why do I need to enter my credit card for the trial?

We require a credit card to help keep our platform safe, as our users are children. It also ensures a smooth experience if you choose to continue after the trial – no disruption to your child’s learning. Rest assured, you won’t be billed until the end of the 7-day trial, and we’ll send you reminders before any charges are made.

I already have a KooBits school account. Can I still try the premium version?

Absolutely! You can still enjoy the full trial experience. In fact, we offer exclusive discounts for school users. After you enter your credit card details during sign-up, you’ll be prompted to log in with your school account to unlock these discounts.

I have more than one child – can they share an account?

We don’t recommend it. KooBits provides personalised content based on each child’s progress, strengths, and weaknesses. To get the most benefit, each child should have their own profile.
