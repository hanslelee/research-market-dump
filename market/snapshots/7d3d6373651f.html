KooBits Is Used In 2/3 Of Singapore Schools

KooBits uses the Singapore Math Method that helps children learn math by using the Concrete-Pictorial-Abstract (CPA) approach. They begin with hands-on experiences (concrete), followed by visual representations like bar models (pictorial), and finally mathematical symbols and equations (abstract).
Singapore Math helps children understand the "why" behind the numbers - making it easier to solve complex problems confidently.
