The Personalised Learning Plan (PLP)
The Personalised Learning Plan (PLP) is a monthly, data-driven roadmap built around two things that matter most: the topics appearing in your child's next exam and the specific gaps holding their score back.
Every lesson, every worksheet and every CAMPUS session follows that plan. Nothing is ever wasted. Unlike regular once-a-week tuition, your child is learning and making progress every day, with every step of the week purposefully connected to the next.
This is not tuition. This is a structured academic improvement system.

Questions Parents Ask
What is Geniebook CAMPUS?
CAMPUS is Geniebook's personalised physical tutoring programme. Every session is built around your child's individual learning needs rather than a fixed syllabus shared with other students. Your child works directly with a qualified teacher who follows a monthly Personalised Learning Plan (PLP) designed specifically for them.
How is CAMPUS different from regular tuition or group classes?
In standard tuition in Singapore, all students follow the same lesson regardless of their individual gaps. CAMPUS works differently. Before each month begins, we use data to identify exactly which topics need the most attention. Our teachers then adjust their pace and explanation style to suit your child, offering a more tailored experience than a typical tuition centre in Singapore.
What does a typical CAMPUS session look like?
Each session follows a structured 3-part format. The teacher starts by reviewing any mistakes from GenieSmart, then works through your child's priority topics with guided practice and feedback. The session closes with a short recap to consolidate what was learned. Every minute is purposeful.
What is the Personalised Learning Plan (PLP)?
The PLP is a monthly roadmap that shows exactly which topics your child will focus on and why. It covers your child's upcoming exam topics and learning gaps, generated from their diagnostic data and updated every month as they progress. You will always know what your child is working on and why.
How many sessions does my child attend each month?
Each CAMPUS session runs for 1.5 hours, once a week. Beyond the weekly session, your child follows a structured 4-step learning cycle throughout the week to keep them engaged and progressing consistently. This is what sets CAMPUS apart from traditional once-a-week tuition.
Is CAMPUS conducted online or in-person?
While we offer online tuition programme, CAMPUS sessions are conducted in person at any of our 7 learning centres across Singapore.
Can CAMPUS help my child prepare for upcoming exams?
Yes. Whether you are looking for PSLE tuition or general exam prep, the PLP is adjusted to prioritise your child's specific exam dates and topics tested. This ensures they focus on what they need for their exams rather than a generic schedule.
How soon can I expect to see results?
Most families see measurable progress in test papers and school results within 2 to 3 months of consistent attendance. Improvement is often visible in test scores, confidence and how students approach academic challenges by targeting the identified gaps.
How do I track my child's progress?
You will receive regular progress updates through the Geniebook app. The PLP also shows your child's movement across topics month by month, so you always have a clear picture of where they stand. Your teacher is available to discuss progress and adjust the learning focus as needed.
Who are the CAMPUS teachers?
All Geniebook teachers go through a rigorous 6-stage hiring process. Every CAMPUS teacher is trained to deliver personalised learning, monitor your child's comprehension in real time and adapt their teaching approach to the individual student.
What subjects does CAMPUS cover?
CAMPUS covers English, Chinese, Mathematics and Science for Primary 1 to Secondary 4 students.
My child is already attending GenieClass or GenieSmart. Can they join CAMPUS too?
Yes. CAMPUS is designed to work alongside Geniebook's other learning programmes. The PLP is built to complement, not duplicate, what your child is already covering in their other classes.
