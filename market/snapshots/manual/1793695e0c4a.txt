The ALS provides a personalised learning pathway for each student.
It is currently available for Primary 5 and 6 Mathematics and Foundation Mathematics, for the topics of Whole Numbers, Decimals, Percentage and Fractions.
More topics, levels and subjects will be added progressively.
Enables self-directed learning where students can access ALS on their own to learn.
Supports monitoring of progress through the Learning Progress Dashboard which provides teachers with a summary of students' concept mastery to inform their interventions, which will help them close students' learning gaps.
